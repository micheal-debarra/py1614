Variable sorter

PY1614 Research Analysis · Lecture 2

Four studies. For every variable, choose what type it is and whether it was measured or manipulated. You'll see straight away whether you were right, and why. Your first choice is the one that counts.

  • Categorical: a set of values with no meaningful order.
  • Ordinal: a meaningful order, but the gaps aren't equal.
  • Numeric: ordered, with equal gaps. Discrete takes whole numbers only; continuous can take values in between.
  • Measured means the researcher recorded it; manipulated means the researcher decided it.
